## Outline

### H2: Why Your Google Rankings Tell You Nothing About AI Visibility
- Google ranks pages; LLMs synthesize and decide which brands to mention
- Average brand appears in <40% of non-branded AI queries
- Three dimensions: frequency, position, sentiment

### H2: Step 1 — Build Your Query Set
- 15-25 queries from VOC, search data, and category/comparison prompts
- Categorize by type, funnel stage, intent
- 50/50 branded vs. non-branded split

### H2: Step 2 — Run Queries Across Three Platforms
- ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini comparison table
- Testing protocol: new conversation, 2x runs, copy full response

### H2: Step 3 — Record Results in a Structured Spreadsheet
- 12-column tracking template
- Dropdown validation + conditional formatting

### H2: Step 4 — Calculate the Three Metrics That Matter
- Mention Frequency (overall, per platform, per funnel stage)
- Share of Voice vs. competitors
- Average Position

### H2: Step 5 — Benchmark Against Competitors
- Run same queries for top 2-3 competitors
- Competitive comparison table (executive deliverable)

### H2: Step 6 — Identify Gaps and Prioritize Fixes
- Decision-stage invisibility → comparison content
- Platform-specific gaps → citation source targeting
- Sentiment issues → third-party validation
- Overall gap → topical authority clusters

### H2: What About LLMs.txt Files?
- No confirmed platform support
- Low-cost experiment, not core strategy

### H2: Repeat Every 90 Days
- Quarterly re-runs measure optimization impact
- Links to citation monitoring and monitoring platforms
